Maya's mission

Opening the court to everyone.

Tennis should be an opportunity, not a privilege. For two years, Maya has been donating her time to make that true for kids in her community.

Why Tikkun Tennis

More than a game.

Tikkun Tennis is a Jewish-founded initiative inspired by the idea that our communities grow stronger when more young people have the chance to participate, learn, and belong.

Tennis can be expensive to start. Equipment, lessons, and club fees can make the sport feel out of reach. Maya, a nationally ranked junior player, shares the same skills she uses in national competition so more kids can pick up a racket, try something new, and enjoy the game.

The program began by serving Jewish kids, and its welcome is universal. You do not need to be Jewish to join. Kids from any background, especially those who might not otherwise get the chance to play, are welcome.
